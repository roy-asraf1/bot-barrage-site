Bot Barrage — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Bot Barrage ("the game") has no user accounts and we, the developer, do not collect or store any personal data on our own servers. The game shows advertising provided through Unity LevelPlay, and that service does process some data, as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Your level progress and settings are saved only on your own device. They are removed when you delete the game or use "Reset progress" in Settings.

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

  • The game shows ads between levels and optional rewarded ads. They are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource Mobile Ltd., a Unity Technologies company) and its advertising partners.
  • To deliver, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Unity and its partners may collect and process: your device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and your interactions with ads.
  • On iOS the advertising identifier is used only if you choose "Allow" in the system tracking prompt. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised using that identifier.
  • Players in Europe are asked whether to allow personalised ads before any ad is requested. Every player can change that choice at any time in the game under Settings → "Ad privacy choices".
  • You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs other than the advertising SDK described above.
  • We do not sell personal data.
