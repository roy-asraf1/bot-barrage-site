Bot Barrage — Support

Questions, bug reports or feedback? Email support@nakama-software.com and include your device model and the level number if it is about a specific level.

Common questions

How do I get more lives? One life comes back every 20 minutes, up to 5. You can also watch an optional ad for 3 lives.

How do I unlock the next zone? Clear 20 levels in the current zone.

How do I change ad privacy choices? In the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices.

How do I reset my progress? Settings → Reset progress. Progress is stored only on your device.
